EverGlow Privacy Policy

From SkyeHub · Last updated October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EverGlow ("EverGlow," "we," "us") handles information when you use the EverGlow Android app and related services. EverGlow is operated through SkyeHub. For questions or privacy requests, email skye@skyehub.org.

1. Information we collect

InformationExamples and purpose
Account and profile informationEmail address, account/user ID, username, display name, and any optional profile details you provide, such as a bio or pronouns.
Messages and social connectionsMessages you send within EverGlow, conversation membership, friends and friend requests, and related timestamps needed to deliver and display conversations.
ImagesProfile pictures, banners, and other images you choose to upload through supported app features.
Notifications and technical informationPush notification tokens and technical identifiers necessary to deliver notifications and operate the service. Our service providers may process device, connection, and diagnostic information in providing their services.
Calling features, if usedIf you use EverGlow's calling features, audio and connection-related information may be transmitted through the calling service to connect participants. We do not claim calls are recorded or permanently stored; this should be verified against the production configuration.

We do not require your physical address, payment card information, or phone contacts to create a standard EverGlow account. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your public profile.

2. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and delete user accounts.
  • Show profiles, deliver messages and images, and manage friendships.
  • Deliver requested notifications and support calling features where available.
  • Maintain service reliability, troubleshoot problems, and protect accounts and the service from misuse.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not describe EverGlow as selling your personal information or using it for targeted advertising. If these practices change, this policy must be updated.

3. Sharing and service providers

Information you choose to put on your profile may be visible to other EverGlow users, depending on the app's features. Messages are delivered to their intended participants. You control what you choose to send or upload.

EverGlow uses third-party infrastructure and services to operate, including Cloudflare for backend and storage services, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, and LiveKit for calling features where enabled. These providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services, subject to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information if legally required or necessary to protect the security of users or the service. We do not knowingly sell personal information.

4. Security

EverGlow uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for supported network communications and authentication measures to help protect accounts. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your password with anyone, including support staff.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account and service information while it is needed to provide EverGlow and operate its features. Some information may remain temporarily in backups, service-provider systems, or records retained where required for legal, security, or operational reasons. Retention periods depend on the data and the applicable systems.

Delete your EverGlow account in the app

  1. Open EverGlow and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Press and hold Delete Account for 10 seconds.
  4. Confirm that you want to delete the account.
  5. Enter your password and complete the deletion request.

Request account deletion without the app

Email skye@skyehub.org from the email address registered to your EverGlow account, with the subject EverGlow Account Deletion. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Never send your password by email.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated data controlled by EverGlow. Information that other participants retain independently (for example, content they copied or saved) cannot necessarily be removed by EverGlow. Some records may be retained where legally required or necessary for security and compliance.

For additional help, see EverGlow Support.

6. Your choices

You can choose what optional profile information and images to provide, edit supported profile details, and request account deletion. You may also manage EverGlow notification permissions in your device settings. To ask about your personal information, contact us at the address below.

7. Children's privacy

EverGlow's intended audience and applicable age restrictions are determined by its published store listing and service rules. If you believe information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable requirements, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, providers, or data practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page when we make changes.
